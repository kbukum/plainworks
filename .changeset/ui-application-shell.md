Add the pieces for a complete app frame. `AppShell` gives you a header, a navigation rail or drawer, a skip link, and a main landmark that takes focus on page change. `NavList` marks the current page and works with any router link, and `StatusBadge` shows a status in one of five tones.

**Breaking:** `ThemeToggle` is replaced by `ThemeModeMenu`, a compact header menu, and `ThemeModeGroup`, an inline button group. Both accept your own icons and labels. Drawer content now scrolls inside the drawer, and table cells hyphenate long words before breaking them.
